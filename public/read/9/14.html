LIBER IX · CAPUT 14

HOW MANY KINDS OF FISH THERE ARE

Latine · Mayhoff 1906

Pilo carentium duo omnino animal pariunt, delphinus ac vipera.

Piscium species sunt LXXIIII, praeter crustis intecta, quae sunt XXX. de singulis alias dicemus; nune enim naturae tractantur insignium.

English · Bostock & Riley 1855–57

There are only two animals without hair that are viviparous, the dolphin and the viper.

There are seventy-four species of fishes, exclusive of those that are covered with crusts; the kinds of which are thirty in number. We shall, on another occasion, speak of each individually; but, for the present, we shall treat only of the nature of the more remarkable ones.
