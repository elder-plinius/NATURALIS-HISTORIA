LIBER X · CAPUT 30

THE ŒNANTHE, THE CHLORION, THE BLACKBIRD, AND THE IBIS

Latine · Mayhoff 1906

merulae circa Cyllenen Arcadiae, nec usquam aliubi, candidae nascuntur. ibis circa Pelusium tantum nigra est, ceteris omnibus locis candida.

English · Bostock & Riley 1855–57

The blackbird is found in the vicinity of Cyllene, in Arcadia, with white plumage; a thing that is the case nowhere else. The ibis, in the neighbourhood of Pelusium only is black, while in all other places it is white.
