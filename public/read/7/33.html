LIBER VII · CAPUT 33

DIVINATION

Latine · Mayhoff 1906

Divinitas et quaedam caelitum societas nobilissima ex feminis in Sibylla fuit, ex viris in Melampode apud Graecos, apud Romanos in Marcio.

English · Bostock & Riley 1855–57

A spirit of divination, and a certain communion with the gods, of the most exalted nature, was manifested—among women, in the Sibyl, and among men, in Melampodes, the Greek, and in Marcius, the Roman.
