# Community code of conduct

We want this project to welcome careful readers, classicists, designers,
engineers, translators, artists, and curious newcomers.

Be generous with questions, precise with evidence, and respectful of people.
Disagreement about a text, translation, historical claim, or design decision
is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing,
and sustained personal attacks are not.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation
that undermine a safe, constructive community. Serious or repeated violations
may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Report conduct concerns privately
to the repository owner; do not force a harmed person to litigate the incident
in a public issue.

This project presents ancient and nineteenth-century material that can contain
offensive or harmful claims. Quoting or analyzing source material in context is
not the same as directing that language at another contributor.
